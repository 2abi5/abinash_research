# Nature (Article)

> Verified 2026-09-30 against <https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors/formatting-guide>
> and the initial-submission guide. Each Nature-family journal differs — check the
> specific journal. Re-verify per `VERIFY.md`.

## Limits

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Total length | ~**2,500 words** (summary paragraph plus body) for a 6-page Article with 4 modest display items; ~**4,300 words** for an 8-page Article with 5–6 display items |
| Summary paragraph | ideally **≤200 words**, fully referenced, separate from the main text |
| References | guideline of **no more than 50** |
| Anonymity | single-blind; double-blind on request |

**Initial submissions are format-flexible** — within reason, style and length will
not influence consideration. Format strictly at revision. Do not spend a week on
formatting before you know the paper is in review.

## The summary paragraph

Not an abstract in the conference sense. It is a fully referenced standalone
paragraph, and it must avoid numbers, abbreviations, acronyms, and measurements
unless essential. Structure it as: broad context accessible to a non-specialist,
the specific gap, what you did, the key result in words rather than numbers, and
the implication.

Writing it well is the single highest-leverage hour in a Nature submission, because
editors triage on it and most submissions are rejected without review.

## Required artifacts

- **Reporting summary** — required for life sciences and behavioural & social
  sciences research, made available to editors and reviewers.
- **Data availability statement** — required.
- **Code availability statement** — required where code is central to the
  conclusions.

## What this venue rewards

- A result that changes what a broad scientific audience believes, stated in terms
  that audience can follow. Technical excellence alone does not clear editorial
  triage.
- Figures that carry the argument. Nature figures are read as the paper.
- Methods that are complete and separate — the Methods section is unlimited in
  practice and is where rigor lives while the body carries the story.

## What gets rejected here

- Most submissions, without review, on editorial assessment of scope and interest.
  This is the normal case, not a failure of the paper.
- Incremental advances, however solid.
- Overclaimed generality — Nature's referees are selected to test exactly this.
- A summary paragraph that only a specialist can read.

## Note on Nature-family journals

*Nature Methods*, *Nature Machine Intelligence*, *Scientific Reports*, and the rest
each have their own word limits, content types, and requirements. Do not carry these
numbers across. Read the target journal's own guide.
