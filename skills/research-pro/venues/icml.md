# ICML 2026

> Verified 2026-09-30 against <https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorInstructions>
> and <https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/LLM-Policy>. Re-verify per `VERIFY.md`.

## Hard limits

| Rule | Value | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Main body | **8 pages** | **Automatic rejection** |
| Excluded from limit | references, impact statement, appendices | — |
| **Impact statement** | **required**, own section at the end with acknowledgements, before references | Required |
| Anonymity | double-blind; no links to non-anonymous repositories | Desk reject |

Submit as a single file. The 8-page rule is enforced automatically — not by a
reviewer's judgment. There is no margin.

## Impact statement

Required, does not count against the limit, and graded as content. Cover the
work's potential broader impact including ethical aspects and future societal
consequences. A generic paragraph about the responsible use of AI is recognizable
and counts against you. Write the specific analysis: who could be harmed, by what
mechanism, what mitigations exist, and which risks you cannot mitigate.

## AI / LLM policy

- LLM assistance for writing or research is **allowed**; all responsibility rests
  with the authors.
- LLMs may **not** be listed as authors and may not be credited as such.
- **Prompt injection is strictly forbidden** — a submission containing it is
  rejected outright.
- In the 2026 cycle ICML desk-rejected **497 submissions** over LLM review-policy
  violations by authors who had signed up as reciprocal reviewers under a
  no-LLM stance and then used one. If you review for ICML, the policy you agreed to
  binds your own submissions.

## Code

Encouraged, and reproducibility is explicitly weighed in decisions. Submit the code
itself or an anonymized repository — never a link that identifies you.

## What this venue rewards

- Claims that are clearly stated and supported by reproducible experiments or sound
  theory. This is the stated bar, near-verbatim.
- Positioning against the broader literature, with the differences from prior work
  made explicit rather than implied.
- Theory with honest assumptions, or experiments with honest protocols. Both are
  welcome; pretending one is the other is not.

## What gets rejected here

- 8.2 pages.
- Impact statement missing or obviously boilerplate.
- A de-anonymizing GitHub link — a genuinely common and entirely avoidable loss.
- Claims that outrun the evidence. ICML reviewers are explicitly directed here.
