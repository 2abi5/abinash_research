# NeurIPS 2026

> Verified 2026-09-30 against <https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers>
> and the Main Track Handbook. Re-verify per `VERIFY.md` before Gate 3.

## Hard limits

| Rule | Value | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Main content | **9 pages**, figures included | Not reviewed, not considered |
| Excluded from limit | acknowledgements, references, checklist, optional technical appendices | — |
| **Paper Checklist** | **mandatory**, placed after references and after any supplemental material | **Desk reject** |
| Anonymity | double-blind | Desk reject |

The checklist is the trap. It is not a formality, it does not count against your
page limit, and papers without it are desk-rejected before any reviewer sees them.
Fill it honestly — answering "yes" to a claim the paper does not support is worse
than answering "no", because area chairs read it against the paper.

## AI / LLM policy

- LLM assistance is permitted; **authors remain fully responsible** for all text,
  figures, and references, and must ensure outputs are correct and original.
- LLMs may not be authors.
- **Methodologically significant or non-standard use of LLMs or agents must be
  disclosed in the paper.** If a model was part of your method or your analysis
  pipeline, that is methodologically significant.
- The Position Paper Track operates a **different** policy. Check it separately.

## What this venue rewards

- A claim-evidence relationship a skeptical reviewer cannot dent. NeurIPS reviewers
  are instructed toward rigor over novelty, and the checklist institutionalizes it.
- Honest limitations. The checklist asks directly, and a paper whose limitations
  section is empty while the checklist claims otherwise reads badly.
- Theoretical claims with stated assumptions and complete proofs (appendix is fine).
- Reproducibility: code, seeds, compute disclosed. The checklist asks for all of it.

## What gets rejected here

- Missing checklist — before review.
- Nine pages of content plus a tenth page of "extra results". Reviewers report it.
- Claims in the abstract that the experiments do not reach.
- Benchmark-only contributions with no analysis of *why* the number moved.
- Undisclosed methodologically significant LLM use.

## Process notes

- Abstract and full-paper deadlines are typically the same date but confirm.
- Rebuttal period with reviewer discussion; a strong rebuttal changes outcomes at
  NeurIPS more than at most venues, so keep an experiment in reserve if you can.
- Supplementary material is optional and reviewers are not obliged to read it.
  Nothing load-bearing goes there.
