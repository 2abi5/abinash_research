# ICLR 2026

> Verified 2026-09-30 against <https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuide>,
> the Reviewer Guide, and <https://iclr.cc/FAQ/LLM>. Re-verify per `VERIFY.md`.

## Hard limits

| Rule | Value | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Main text at submission | **9 pages or fewer** | Desk reject |
| Rebuttal / camera-ready | raised to **10 pages** for new results and discussion | — |
| Excluded from limit | references (unlimited), appendices | — |
| Anonymity | double-blind, on OpenReview | Desk reject |

Plan for 9 and keep the 10th page in reserve for the rebuttal. Reviewers at ICLR
expect new experiments during discussion, and having the page for them is an
advantage.

## AI / LLM policy — the strictest surface here

- **Any significant LLM use in research ideation or writing must be disclosed.**
- Undisclosed use found during discussion: reviewers are instructed to notify the
  area chair.
- **LLM-generated papers have been desk-rejected outright**, publicly.
- Reviewers must disclose LLM use in their reviews via a dedicated form field;
  failing to do so puts the reviewer's *own* papers at risk of desk rejection.
- The governing principle is the Code of Ethics line that all contributions to the
  research must be acknowledged.

Practically: write the disclosure, put it in the paper, and make it accurate. See
`../references/18-disclosure-and-ethics.md`. This is a venue where the disclosure
is cheaper than the risk by orders of magnitude.

## What this venue rewards

- Open, public review. Your paper and its reviews are visible, and so is your
  rebuttal. Reviewers reward authors who engage substantively.
- Clear writing — ICLR's public forum means a badly written paper is badly
  reviewed in public.
- Novel representations and learning insights; the venue's identity is
  representation learning.
- Engagement during the discussion period. Silence loses papers here that would be
  accepted with a rebuttal.

## What gets rejected here

- Undisclosed LLM use.
- Over 9 pages at submission.
- No rebuttal, or a rebuttal that concedes nothing and adds nothing.
- Claims that reviewers can check against the public record and find wanting.
